Hi there, here is how Merced County evaluates commercial kitchens.

Who Inspects You

Food safety: Merced County Department of Public Health — Division of Environmental Health

Fire safety: City of Merced Fire Department (city limits); CAL FIRE MMU (unincorporated)

How This County Evaluates

This county uses a point-accumulation system with tiered ratings to evaluate food safety inspections.

Unique point-accumulation system. Points accumulate UPWARD per violation: critical=4pts, major=2pts, minor=1pt. Good (0-6 pts), Satisfactory (7-13 pts), Unsatisfactory (14+ pts). NO letter grades. Grade card posted at facility. Award of Excellence for facilities with zero major violations across all routine inspections.

Hood Cleaning Frequency

This county enforces Every 6 months hood cleaning as the default schedule.

Your specific frequency depends on cooking volume (NFPA 96 Table 12.4):

Hood / Cooking TypeFrequency
Type I — Heavy volumeMonthly
Type I — Moderate volumeQuarterly
Type I — Low volumeEvery 6 months
Type II hoodAnnually
Solid fuel cookingMonthly
